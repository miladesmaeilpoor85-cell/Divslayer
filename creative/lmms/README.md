# Divslayer title-theme sketch 01

Editable source: `divslayer_title_theme_sketch_01.mmp`

## Musical draft

- Eight-bar motif at 108 BPM
- D-Hijaz-flavoured melodic material with a low drone
- LMMS 1.2.x native instruments (Triple Oscillator)
- Intended as a short title-theme motif study, not a finished soundtrack cue

## Measured render review

The first CI render was 44.1 kHz, stereo, 16-bit PCM, approximately 20 seconds including around 2.1 seconds of trailing digital silence. Technical measurement of that raw render:

- Integrated loudness: approximately -9.8 LUFS
- True peak: approximately -4.0 dBFS
- Clipped samples: zero
- Dynamic range (EBU LRA): approximately 6.6 LU

The raw render is substantially louder than the project's existing soundtrack targets (typically around -18 to -17.5 LUFS). The creative pipeline therefore creates a **separate** listening preview by removing trailing digital silence, applying a short fade, and matching the integrated level to -18.5 LUFS. The raw LMMS export remains unchanged.

## Approval status

This remains a technical and compositional draft. No human listening approval has been recorded. It is not assigned to the title screen or any game scene, and it is not included in production audio. Before shipping, review the melody, arrangement, loop/transition behavior, mobile playback, and the terms for any bundled LMMS waveform content.
