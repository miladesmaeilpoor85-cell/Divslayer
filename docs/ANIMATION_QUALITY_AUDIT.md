# Frame-level animation audit

The animation quality audit is a read-only CI tool. It reads `data/art_manifest.json` and the registered PNG artwork, then writes a JSON report under `artifacts/animation-review/`. It never modifies game assets or the manifest.

## Measurements

- Frame count and unique-frame count for each directional hero clip and enemy variant.
- Exact duplicate frame pairs and the fraction of adjacent pixels that visibly change.
- Average non-transparent alpha coverage and variation in the visible bottom edge as a footline-drift review cue.
- Attack FPS, contact-frame index, and contact time when the manifest contains those fields.

The audit understands embedded attack columns, separate attack atlases, and per-direction `attackFrameFiles`. The separate-frame format is currently used by Warrior Starter; the Base64 attack-atlas format is currently used by Rogue Starter.

## Interpretation

A one-frame attack is reported as a single-pose clip, not automatically as a bug. Two-frame and low-motion warnings are prompts for visual review. Alpha coverage and pixel differences do not measure pose quality, weight, appeal, weapon readability, or correct acting. Those require human review at native sprite size and actual Android display scale.

## Current manifest baseline — 21 November 2026

There are 12 registered hero looks and three directional attack clips per look, for 36 directional clips total.

- **Warrior Starter:** three four-frame directional clips.
- **Rogue Starter:** three four-frame directional clips.
- **Single-pose clips:** the other 30 directional clips across Warrior Mid/Endgame, Mage Starter/Mid/Endgame, Rogue Mid/Endgame, and Paladin Starter/Mid/Endgame.

Therefore, six of the 36 registered directional clips are multi-frame and 30 remain single-pose. This is a count of manifest frame data, not an artistic quality score. The audit JSON generated by CI remains the machine-readable source of truth after future asset updates.

## Current limitation

The six-pose class-selection illustrations are menu presentation art, not directional gameplay sprite sheets. They do not replace the combat animations in this audit. New combat frames must be authored as distinct poses, reviewed for direction/orientation and foot-pivot consistency, registered in the manifest, and tested in the game before the baseline changes.
