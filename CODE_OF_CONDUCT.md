# Code of Conduct | منشور رفتار

Be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

با دیگران محترمانه، سازنده و منصفانه رفتار کنید. آزار، تبعیض، تهدید، حمله شخصی، افشای اطلاعات خصوصی و رفتار توهین‌آمیز پذیرفته نیست.

- Discuss ideas, not personal traits.
- Give actionable and respectful feedback.
- Respect cultural context and different experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused and welcoming.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations. Report concerns privately through GitHub contact options; do not post sensitive details publicly.
